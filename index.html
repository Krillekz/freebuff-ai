Challenger

The rulebook

Five slots, five attributes. Each pulled character belongs on exactly one line: Strength, Speed, Abilities, IQ or Durability.

  • Peak — the card’s authored attribute. A perfect file appends * and multiplies hard.
  • Off-meta — a peak filed out of position collapses to a penalised slot and rates as E.
  • Sealed — peaks are hidden until the deck is locked. Clues live in the signature, archetype and trait lines.
  • No pity — every pull is a flat roll from the 500-series pool.

Live pull odds

8 bands

Eight bands. Every roll is uniform across the 500 series; the printed bands are the share of the pool that lands there. There is no pity counter and no weighted table.

How to build

Read the card, not the colour. The rarity tag is a hint, not a guarantee — a low band with a peak on a rare attribute can carry a slot.

  • 01 Roll until the board offers a character you can place.
  • 02 File blind. The board never shows the peak.
  • 03 Seal, then let the rater reveal what you actually built.
  • 04 Take the same core into 1v1 Bot or a live room.
